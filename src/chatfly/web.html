Zumi 3 (Phase 7f). to means the last thing named (to ke?); properties go in pair order (ice ka bon ku.). The fly says its own reply: a grammar fly reads your sentence, the trip, memory and yes/no flies supply what to say, and a speaking fly samples every word from its output neurons. It can ask back: o. to gu ba? (okay; is it big?).

How it answers. A fixed parser (no LLM) splits your sentence by the one grammar rule. The fly runs each move word through its feedback loop, starting from where it last was, so its memory of its place is the loop itself. The page only names the fly's final place; it never looks at the map.

How it answers. A fixed parser (no LLM) turns your sentence into tokens. The fly then runs them through its feedback loop, one step per token. Each step's decoded state is fed back as input to the next step. The reply comes only from the fly's final state.
